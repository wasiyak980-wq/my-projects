I.An iced Americano is a refreshing, cold coffee beverage made by diluting one or two shots of intense espresso with cold water and serving it over ice.

II.A cappuccinox is an Italian coffee beverage traditionally composed of equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam, usually in a 5–6 oz cup

III. Café au lait(French for "coffee with milk") is a breakfast beverage made by mixing strong brewed coffee with hot or steamed milk in a 1:1 ratio. Unlike a latte which uses espresso, café au lait uses regular coffee, creating a milder, creamy flavor. It can also refer to a light brown, creamy skin tonn.
